1. Project title:  AI/ML-Based Predictive Maintenance and Vehicle Health Monitoring 
System  
AI/ML Technologies: Random Forest, XGBoost, LSTM, XAI 
Models: Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, XGBoost, LSTM, GRU, 
Transformer 
Goal: Predict vehicle/component failure before it occurs. 
Input data 
 Engine temperature  
 RPM  
 Oil pressure  
 Vibration  
 Battery voltage  
 Coolant temperature  
 Fuel consumption  
 Vehicle speed  
 Operating hours 
Architecture  
Vehicle Sensors 
      ↓ 
RPM | Temperature | Pressure | Vibration 
      ↓ 
Data Preprocessing 
      ↓ 
ML/DL Model 
      ↓ 
Failure Probability 
      ↓ 
Vehicle Health Score 
      ↓ 
Maintenance Alert 
Sample Output 
Vehicle Health: 72% 
Failure Probability: 68% 
Status: WARNING 
 
Major contributing factors: 
1. High vibration 
=== PAGE 2 ===
2. Rising temperature 
3. Abnormal oil pressure 
 
2. Project title: AI-Based Driver Behaviour, Fatigue and Risk Detection System 
AI/ML Technologies: ML, CNN, LSTM 
The system learns driving behavior and identifies potentially unsafe situations. 
Parameters 
 Speed  
 Acceleration  
 Braking  
 Steering  
 Lane deviation  
 Eye closure  
 Head position  
 Phone usage 
Develop a system that identifies: 
 Normal driving  
 Aggressive acceleration  
 Sudden braking  
 Distracted driving  
 Drowsiness  
 Unsafe driving 
Architecture  
Camera + Vehicle Sensors 
          ↓ 
Feature Extraction 
          ↓ 
CNN/LSTM 
          ↓ 
Driver Behavior 
          ↓ 
Risk Score 
Possible classes 
 Normal  
 Aggressive  
 Distracted  
 Drowsy  
 Unsafe braking  
 Unsafe acceleration

3. Project title: AI-Based Automotive Review and Customer Sentiment Analytics 
AI/ML Technologies: BERT, XLM-R, NLP 
Goals: Analyze vehicle reviews and identify sentiment for individual aspects. 
Data 
Automotive reviews can be analyzed for: 
 Vehicle  
 Engine  
 Battery  
 Mileage  
 Safety  
 Comfort  
 Service  
 Infotainment  
 Price 
Architecture  
Automotive Reviews 
       ↓ 
Text Preprocessing 
       ↓ 
Tokenizer 
       ↓ 
BERT / XLM-R 
       ↓ 
Sentiment Classification 
       ↓ 
Aspect Extraction 
       ↓ 
Aspect-Level Sentiment 
 
Example: 
"The battery range is excellent, but the charging time is too long." 
Output 
Aspect           Sentiment 
Battery range           Positive 
Charging time           Negative
